01

What CHIMERA is

CHIMERA is a video editing and measurement tool. It composes short-form videos from images, text and audio, renders them, and measures both its own output and reference material along the same set of properties — cut rate, subtitle size, brightness, saturation, grain, loudness, speech density and video length.

Its TikTok integration exists for one purpose: to retrieve the reach of the owner's own published videos, so that those measured properties can be compared against the outcome they actually produced.

05

What the measurements are, and are not

CHIMERA reports measured quantities and the statistical significance of differences between them. It deliberately produces no virality score: a single number on a scale would require weightings that nobody actually knows, and inventing them would present a guess as a finding.

Nothing CHIMERA reports is a prediction, a guarantee of reach, or advice of any kind. Editorial decisions remain entirely the owner's.
